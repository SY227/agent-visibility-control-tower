Screenshots to capture and insert manually

1. Hero / main app screen
   - Capture the opening screen with the title, URL intake, and workflow panel visible.
   - Best placement: Slide 1 hero placeholder.

2. Workflow trace
   - Capture the six-stage workflow trace during or after a run.
   - Best placement: optional support for Slide 4 or Slide 9 checklist reference.

3. Executive Verdict + Boardroom Snapshot
   - Capture the top of the AI Visibility Readiness Brief showing Executive Verdict and Boardroom Snapshot together.
   - Best placement: Slide 5 large top placeholder.

4. Decision Memo + Scorecards
   - Capture the Decision Memo and the scorecards section.
   - Best placement: Slide 5 lower-left placeholder.

5. Fix Pack
   - Capture the Fix Pack section with top actions clearly visible.
   - Best placement: Slide 5 lower-right placeholder.

6. Shopify Executive Verdict
   - Run the Shopify sample case and capture the top summary output.
   - Best placement: Slide 6 upper-right left placeholder.

7. Shopify Fix Pack
   - Capture the Shopify Fix Pack with first-week move and top actions visible.
   - Best placement: Slide 6 upper-right right placeholder.

8. Shopify Evidence Receipts or Agent Artifacts
   - Capture either the Evidence Receipts section or the Visible Agent Artifacts section from the Shopify run.
   - Best placement: Slide 6 wide lower-right placeholder.

9. Final hero or boardroom screenshot
   - Choose the cleanest polished screenshot for the closing slide.
   - Best placement: Slide 9 closing placeholder.

Recommended capture approach
- Use the Shopify sample case for all case-study screenshots.
- Capture at full desktop width so the cards feel premium and readable.
- Prefer screenshots with generous whitespace and no browser chrome if possible.
- Replace placeholders after final copy review, then export the submission deck.
